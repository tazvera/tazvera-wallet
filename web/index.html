SAFETY FIRST

Tazvera Shield checks every send.

  • Look-alike addresses: scammers copy the first and last letters. Shield spots the trick and stops the send.
  • Wrong places: burn and token-contract addresses are blocked, so coins are not lost.
  • Your 12 words never leave your phone. Tazvera never asks for them. Anyone who does is a scammer.
